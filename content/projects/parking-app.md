parkQu is a parking service designed for Queen's University students without parking passes. Think Airbnb, but for driveways, lots, and any private patch of ground a car fits on.

## Process

- **User-centred design**: interviews surfaced the user group and the real problem before any screens were drawn.
- **High-fidelity prototyping in Figma**, iterated through multiple rounds of testing.
- The result stays tightly scoped to its mission: let students book spots near campus from people with spare space.

The project demonstrates planning a large-scale application around a niche problem without straying from the original mission statement.
